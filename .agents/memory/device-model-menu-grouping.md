---
name: Device model menu grouping
description: Defines scan-friendly grouping for large device-model menus.
---

Large device families must be grouped in the first visible shortlist as well as
the expanded overview. Samsung separates Galaxy S, A, Z/Fold/Flip, Note, M, J,
XCover and remaining models. iPhone separates numbered generations, the
X/XR/XS generation, SE and earlier models.

**Why:** a globally newest-first grid mixes unrelated product lines. Customers
scan by Samsung series or iPhone generation, so the mixed list is slow to read
even when its chronology is technically correct.

**How to apply:** keep sections in a stable commercial order, retain
newest-to-oldest ordering inside each section, preserve every model when
expanded, and keep typed search as one relevance-ranked result list. Make model
search the visually primary path. Browsing starts with collapsed series cards;
open one relevant series and show only a short newest-first list. Expanding a
series must be explicit and must not expand unrelated series. Scrolling or
swiping never means “show every model.”

Primary brand order is Apple then Samsung. Other device brands belong after the
part departments under the final other-brands menu, not as competing top-level
items. Numbered suffix variants inherit their base generation (for example,
iPhone 16e belongs to iPhone 16). When placement is uncertain, follow the
established grouping in the previous shop rather than inventing a new hierarchy.
