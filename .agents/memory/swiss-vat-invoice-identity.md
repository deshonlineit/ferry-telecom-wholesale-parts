---
name: Swiss VAT invoice identity
description: Legal identity and tax-field rules for Ferry Telecom invoices under Swiss VAT Act Article 26.
---

Invoices must clearly identify the supplier, recipient, nature and extent of the supply, relevant date, consideration, VAT rate, and VAT amount. Show the seller’s VAT registration number and the recipient’s real business identity and location.

**Why:** Swiss VAT Act Article 26 makes clear identification and these core supply/tax fields the basis of a compliant invoice. A recipient VAT number or internal customer number is not a substitute for name and location.

**How to apply:** Use the immutable order address for the recipient, add real account email and registration data only when present, and never synthesize a customer number or missing tax identifier.
