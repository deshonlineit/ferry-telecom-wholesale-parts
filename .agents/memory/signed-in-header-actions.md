---
name: Signed-in header actions
description: Visual and accessibility rule for compact signed-in storefront actions.
---

My account and Sign out are icon-only circular controls in the signed-in
storefront header. Cart remains labelled and keeps its visible item count.

**Why:** the owner finds the account and sign-out text visually noisy and
unnecessarily wide, while Cart benefits from explicit identity and quantity.

**How to apply:** use matching 40px controls with distinct account and exit
icons, accessible names, hover tooltips, and visible keyboard focus. Give sign
out a restrained red hover treatment so its consequence is recognizable.
