---
name: B2B legal policy
description: Commercial boundary for Ferry Telecom's public returns, warranty and legal wording.
---

Keep customer-facing legal and service terms limited to Swiss B2B obligations and explicit Ferry Telecom commitments. Do not add a voluntary change-of-mind period, satisfaction guarantee, lifetime warranty, automatic cash refund, or installation-cost reimbursement. Defect handling requires prompt inspection, prior RMA approval and a seller-selected repair, replacement or credit remedy, while preserving rights that cannot legally be excluded.

**Why:** The owner explicitly chose a conservative Swiss B2B position rather than unusually easy consumer-style returns or warranties.

**How to apply:** Review legal pages, checkout copy, account return flows and marketing claims against this boundary whenever return, warranty, quality or service wording changes.
